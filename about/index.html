About

PMA was born from a simple observation. Too often, incredible careers are built without unlocking the full potential that comes with them. We saw people achieve extraordinary things, win remarkable awards and reach levels most can only dream of, yet the personal brand surrounding their career was often left behind.

We believe that the person behind the name is just as important as the name itself. Our role is to understand what makes each talent unique, highlight the sides of them that people may not know yet and build a personal brand that truly reflects who they are. Any public figure should be able to focus on what they do best, knowing their personal brand is in the right hands.

A career may eventually come to an end, but your personal brand does not. The person you become, the story you build and the impact you leave behind can last a lifetime. That is what PMA believes personal branding should be about.

Our founder

PMA was founded by Robin Nieuwenhuijzen, whose interest in social media and personal branding started long before the agency itself. With a professional football player as her partner, Robin saw up close how much potential exists around a career, while also noticing how often everything outside of the sport itself was overlooked. Even when incredible achievements were being made, there was still so much more that could be built around the person behind them.

This sparked Robin’s curiosity to experiment with what can influence a career, both online and offline. What started as a personal interest quickly grew into a passion for understanding how personality, perception, storytelling and digital presence can shape the way someone is seen and remembered.

She created PMA because she believes talent should not have to worry about everything surrounding their career. They should have an agency they can trust to understand who they really are, both on and off the pitch, stage or set. Someone who can bring out the parts of their personality that deserve to be seen and turn them into a personal brand that feels authentic.

For Robin, it is not about simply making someone more visible. It is about making sure the right things are seen, remembered and carried forward long after the career itself has changed.
