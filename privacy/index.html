Privacy

Your privacy

Personal Media Agency respects your privacy. This statement explains which personal data this website processes, why, and what your rights are.

What we process

When you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, your email address and your message. We use these only to reply to you.

The message is sent to us by email. It is not stored in the database of this website.

Cookies

This website only uses the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie and a security cookie that protects forms. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not use analytics services from third parties.

Who else sees your data

The website and our email are hosted by service providers that process data on our behalf. We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to answer your question and to follow up on our conversation.

Your rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, and you may object to how we use it. Send your request through the contact form. You also have the right to file a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this statement. The most recent version is always on this page.
